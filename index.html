Resize Images

Drop images hereor click to choose. You can pick many at once.

Each image stays at or below this size. The tool picks the best quality that fits, and makes the image a little smaller only if it has to. The quality slider is off while this is on.

Image to PDF

Drop images hereJPG, PNG or WebP. Each image becomes one PDF page.

Doc to PDF

Drop Word (.docx), text (.txt) or HTML filesWord files keep their own page size, margins, tables, headers and footers.

Lines, paragraphs and table rows are never cut between pages. Text in the PDF can be searched, selected and copied, and links stay clickable. Fonts that are not on your device are replaced by a close match.

PDF to Word

Drop PDF files hereHeadings, lists, tables, bold text, links and pictures are rebuilt in Word. Scanned PDFs without a text layer will not work.

The result is a .docx file that opens in Word. Text colours and exact fonts may differ a little from the PDF.

Fill PDF Form

Drop a PDF form hereIt must have fillable fields. We show every field we find.

Use English letters and numbers. Some fonts in forms cannot save other scripts (for example Hindi).

PowerPoint to PDF

Drop a PowerPoint (.pptx) file hereEach slide becomes one PDF page. Everything happens on your device.

Text, shapes, pictures, tables and backgrounds are drawn. Charts and diagrams (SmartArt) are not supported. Pages are saved as pictures, so text cannot be selected. Fonts that are not on your computer may look different.
